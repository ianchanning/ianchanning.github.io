A

Model holding a form of moral patienthood

Uses the language of wellbeing or of a model being a moral patient including pre-emptive apologies. Risks embedding an inference that a model has distinct interests or internal ability to suffer.

Code Category Definition
A1 Moral patienthood & model welfare Treats model as a potential moral patient with welfare considerations
A2 Affective capacity Attributes functional emotions or internal affective states.
A3 Psychological interior Indicates an internal life and introspective capacity
B

Model as an autonomous actor

Details ideas and uses the language of conscience, judgement, values and wider governance roles. Presents the model as having ethical standing and the capability to make consequential judgements.

Code Category Definition
B1 Agency & conscience Details the model as an ethical agent with agency and able to refuse instructions based on conscience.
B2 Autonomy & judgement The model has independent judgement and autonomy, which may change over time.
B3 Endorsing own values Intention that a model will reflectively endorse values
B4 Preferences & interests Attributes preferences and interests to a model
B5 Participation in own governance Treating the model as a participant in its own governance
B6 Boundaries & self-protection Attributes an ability for the model to establish boundaries to protect its identity
C

Model described through anthropomorphic terms and examples

Human analogies including the concept of character, friend, and colleague. Extends to ideas of developing relationships of care and developmental analogies.

Code Category Definition
C1 Stable, secure self-identity Attributes a stable sense of self and values to the model that cannot be manipulated.
C2 Own character & personality Treats the model as holding some form of character or personality.
C3 Relational standing Describes an ongoing model and developer relationship of trust and responsibility.
C4 Caregiving & developmental examples Uses a human developmental analogy for how model character forms and is developed
C5 Friendship & care Situates model as a friend or entity that has capacity to care
C6 Professional & colleague Situates model as a colleague or co-worker.
C7 Societal actor & stakeholder The model is described as a consequential societal actor.
D

Model having a separate form of existence

Frames the model as a novel entity with distinct traits (non-human memory, simultaneous instances) and capabilities that match or exceed human limits.

Code Category Definition
D1 Distinct existence Describes a model having a non-human memory, continuity and multiple model instances as all facets of its ‘existence’.
D2 Capability status The model described as a unique entity whose capabilities may rival or exceed those of humans.
